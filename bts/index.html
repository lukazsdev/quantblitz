trading · brain teasersoptimal stopping

You roll a fair die and are paid its value in dollars, with the option to re-roll once and take the second roll. What is the game worth with optimal play?

Keep 4, 5 or 6, which happens half the time and averages 5, and otherwise re-roll for an average of 3.5: (1/2)(5) + (1/2)(3.5) = $4.25. $5.00 forgets that you only get to keep a high roll half the time, and ≈ $4.17 comes from the wrong cutoff, keeping only 5 or 6 or keeping 3 as well.
